HOW IT WORKS

A steady path from brief to better draft.

A clear process helps keep the work manageable, transparent, and firmly connected to your own academic voice.

01 / BRIEF

Share the context

Describe your assignment, aims, discipline, draft stage, and the kind of feedback that would be useful.

02 / PLANNING

Set a useful scope

Agree priorities, a realistic delivery window, and a learner-first support plan before any review begins.

03 / REVIEW

Receive focused notes

Use clear observations, questions, and recommendations to understand what deserves your attention next.

04 / COLLABORATION

Discuss the thinking

Bring questions to the conversation and decide how each suggestion fits your own argument and voice.

05 / DELIVERY

Work from a clear record

Feedback is organised so you can track decisions, distinguish essential edits, and preserve authorship.

06 / REFINEMENT

Make the final decisions

You revise your work, apply what helps, and remain responsible for meeting your institution’s policies.

ABOUT DRAFTORBIT

Academic support built around better decisions.

DraftOrbit is an original academic-support concept centred on thoughtful questions, clear feedback, and a responsible approach to learner authorship.

Clear thinking

We value questions that clarify the task before the drafting pressure takes over.

Responsible support

Our model focuses on feedback, planning, editing, and skills—not producing assessed work.

Team approach

A brief can be matched to an appropriate perspective while the learner remains the author.

Our responsible academic support principle

DraftOrbit is designed as a space for learning conversations. We do not ghostwrite, complete assessed assignments, fabricate research, or misrepresent authorship. Learners decide what to revise and are responsible for their academic work.
